BEFORE WE BEGIN

Good questions.

Are UGC films and avatar videos different?

Yes. UGC ad films are product and brand stories for campaigns. Avatar videos use an approved human clone for your recurring personal content. They have separate packages and creative approaches.

What is included in PinYourSite?

Architect’s Cut includes a complete 3D informational web view and the project information your client needs. We agree the project plan, plot data, gallery, location details and enquiry flow before building.

What does Fox’s Cut include?

A complete working AI software product, application or website in 20 days, based on the agreed scope and supplied inputs. Websites include lifetime hosting. Domains, external services, maintenance and additional scope are confirmed in your proposal.

What do you need for a human clone?

The person’s permission, suitable reference footage, approved topics and language preferences. AI presenters are not customer endorsements. Product claims and scripts need your approval.

How do we book a project?

Choose a cut or send a brief on WhatsApp. We confirm scope, taxes, timeline, revisions and payment terms together. The site does not collect payments.
