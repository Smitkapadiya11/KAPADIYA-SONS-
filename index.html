A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Good questions.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?+

No. Start with your business goal or the task that is taking too much time. The free review identifies 2–3 practical opportunities before we discuss a paid scope.

Are the UGC presenters real customers?+

AI UGC is creator-style marketing content. AI presenters do not imply genuine customer experience. Scripts should use verified product facts, and any endorsement or customer review needs to be genuine and approved.

What do you need to create my avatar?+

The depicted person’s permission, suitable reference footage and approved talking points. We confirm reference requirements, language and creative direction before production.

Can you build a custom system for my business?+

Yes. Websites, apps, CRM, WhatsApp workflows and automation are scoped around your needs. Features, integrations, third-party fees, support and timelines are agreed in a custom proposal.

Are package prices the final payable amount?+

The listed prices reflect the Diwali offers, including the fixed ₹4,000 Avatar Demo base plan. Taxes, payment terms, revisions, delivery dates and any unspecified details are confirmed before booking. No payment is collected on this site.
